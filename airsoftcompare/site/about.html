About CompAirsoft

What this is

CompAirsoft compares the prices of airsoft guns, magazines, BBs, optics, tactical gear and clothing across Czech airsoft shops. Instead of opening every shop separately, you see all offers for a product side by side, the cheapest first.

Where the prices come from

We read each shop's public product feed or product pages several times a day. Prices, stock and product names come straight from the shops, so they can be a few hours old. Always check the price in the shop before you buy.

We don't sell anything

CompAirsoft is not a shop. An offer takes you straight to the shop's own page; we don't take orders or payments. No shop pays for its position: offers are sorted by price.
